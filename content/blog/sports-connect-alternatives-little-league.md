# Sports Connect Alternatives in 2026: What Little League Admins Actually Need to Know

*Last updated: November 2026*

If you run a Little League and you've heard that Sports Connect is going away, you've probably started searching for alternatives (or for "SportsConnect alternatives," since the name gets spelled both ways). Here's the thing most "Sports Connect alternatives" articles get wrong: **if you're a chartered Little League, the biggest part of that decision has already been made for you.**

This guide explains what's actually happening, what the transition does and doesn't solve, and what your real options are — written for the volunteer board member who has a league to run and no time for vendor marketing.

## What actually happened

Three facts drive everything else:

1. **In June 2025, Stack Sports (Sports Connect's parent company) merged with PlayMetrics**, a youth sports operations company that started with club software and has since expanded into leagues and governing bodies. Private equity firm Genstar Capital backed the merger and is the majority owner of the combined company, with PlayMetrics' CEO leading it. ([Announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250611023323/en/PlayMetrics-and-Stack-Sports-Combine-to-Create-Leader-in-Sports-Software))

2. **Sports Connect is being sunset in 2027.** The combined company plans to retire Sports Connect and the Association Platform, and PlayMetrics says the old platform won't support the 2027 season. ([PlayMetrics' Little League page](https://home.playmetrics.com/little-league-central-registration))

3. **Little League Central Registration is moving to PlayMetrics.** Beginning with the 2027 season, leagues will manage player registration, league operations, and volunteer management in PlayMetrics. ([Sports Connect's Little League page](https://sportsconnect.com/little-league/)) No switch dates for individual leagues have been published, and Little League's policy pages still name Sports Connect as the required platform today. If your league is still on Sports Connect, keep using it until Little League or your District Administrator gives you a date. This was significant enough to get its own education session at the 2026 Little League International Congress. ([Little League Congress details](https://www.littleleague.org/news/looking-back-at-day-two-of-the-2026-little-league-international-congress/), [Sports Connect's Little League page](https://sportsconnect.com/little-league/))

AYSO is making the same move on the soccer side — its regions are also being transitioned to PlayMetrics. ([AYSO announcement](https://ayso.org/ayso-registration-and-region-management-with-playmetrics/))

## What this means if you're a chartered Little League

Because registration for Little League flows through Central Registration, and Central Registration is moving to PlayMetrics, **your registration path for 2027 is essentially decided.** Little League has required US leagues to use Sports Connect for player registration since 2025, through Central Registration. So unlike an independent rec league that can shop the whole market, a chartered Little League isn't really choosing between six registration platforms. Confirm specifics with your District Administrator, but the practical picture is:

- **Registration, league operations, and volunteer management** move to PlayMetrics for the 2027 season. Background checks run through JDP's Sports Connect integration today, and PlayMetrics lists background-screening tracking as "coming soon," so ask your District Administrator how screening works after your switch.
- **Your league website** needs a plan, but don't move it yet. Little League's websites policy ([Little League websites policy](https://www.littleleague.org/playing-rules/position-policy-statements/websites/)) still requires US league websites to be on Sports Connect, and PlayMetrics offers league websites built on Little League templates. Ask your District Administrator before you build or move anything.
- **Your historical data** (past rosters, registrations) should be exported now. There's no published date for when leagues lose access to Sports Connect, so don't wait for one. (AYSO has told its regions they keep look-up access through September 2027; that's AYSO's guidance, not Little League's.)

So the honest question for a Little League admin isn't *"what replaces Sports Connect?"* It's narrower and more useful: **"the registration decision is made — what about everything else?"**

## The part the transition doesn't automatically solve: your season

Registration is a busy stretch once or twice a year. The other pain runs all season long:

- **Field conflicts.** Four divisions, two shared parks, one city permit schedule. One double-booked field ruins a Saturday for eighty families.
- **Interleague play.** Coordinating schedules with neighboring leagues, where every change has to be re-agreed by two boards.
- **Umpire assignments.** Who's behind the plate for the 12s on Sunday, and did anyone tell them the game moved?
- **Rainouts.** One storm and you're rebuilding three weeks of schedule by hand.
- **Snack shack duty.** The rotation nobody wants to manage and everybody complains about.
- **Playoff brackets.** Built in a spreadsheet at 11pm, usually.

PlayMetrics has scheduling tools, including field plans and a shared calendar. Speaking as a Little League board member, the part I'd want to see before trusting any tool is the messy part: shared fields across divisions, interleague games two boards have to agree on, and volunteer umpires. Don't assume the registration migration solves scheduling. Ask PlayMetrics for a demo of the scheduling workflow using your real fields, divisions, and interleague partners.

## Your three realistic paths

### Path 1: Move to PlayMetrics and use it for everything

The default path, and the least work. If PlayMetrics' scheduling handles your league's reality — shared fields across divisions, interleague, officials, rainout rescheduling — this is the simplest answer. Pressure-test it in a demo before your board assumes it. On cost, PlayMetrics says it will honor the current Little League pricing model. If you'd use anything beyond what Central Registration covers, ask about that pricing directly.

### Path 2: All-in-one alternatives (mostly for non-chartered organizations)

If you're an independent league, not bound to Central Registration, the full-replacement market is real. One thing to know first: in May 2026, PlayMetrics bought SportsEngine ([Versant press release, "PlayMetrics Acquires SportsEngine from Versant," May 1, 2026](https://pressroom.versantmedia.com/versant-media/press-releases/playmetrics-acquires-sportsengine-versant)). So two of the biggest names in this market now belong to the same company. If you're leaving Sports Connect to get away from PlayMetrics, SportsEngine isn't a way out.

Here's how each vendor described itself on its own site in November 2026:

- **TeamSnap:** registration and payments, websites, schedules, team communication, and a parent app. League pricing is quoted, not published.
- **SportsEngine (now PlayMetrics):** registration and payments, websites, scheduling, and communication. Pricing is quoted, not published.
- **LeagueApps:** registration, payments, scheduling, communication, and websites. Charges a percentage of each payment instead of a subscription.
- **Jersey Watch:** registration and payments, websites, schedules, and text and email alerts. Publishes an annual plan price; built for volunteers.
- **TeamLinkt:** registration, payments, websites, scheduling, and messaging. Core platform is free; earns on payment processing and paid add-ons.
- **TeamSideline:** registration, scheduling, communication, and team websites, for what it calls a low annual fee.

Check current pricing with each vendor before you decide.

## A sane timeline for the transition

- **Now:** Keep using Sports Connect until you have a switch date. Export your historical data, watch PlayMetrics' training webinar recordings (in the Little League Data Center), and demo the scheduling workflow with your real fields and divisions.
- **Once you have your switch date:** Enroll and verify your league in PlayMetrics, confirm your website and background-check plan with your District Administrator, decide your scheduling plan, and train the two or three volunteers who'll actually run these tools.
- **Your first season on PlayMetrics:** Everything should already be boring by then.

## FAQ

**Is Sports Connect going away?**
Yes. Little League registration moves to PlayMetrics beginning with the 2027 season, and the parent company plans to sunset Sports Connect in 2027. No switch dates for individual leagues have been published, and Little League still requires Sports Connect today, so keep using it until Little League or your District Administrator says otherwise. Export your data now.

**Do Little Leagues get to choose their registration platform?**
No. Little League has required US leagues to use Sports Connect for registration since 2025, through Central Registration, and that registration is moving to PlayMetrics. Your District Administrator will tell you when your league switches.

**Does PlayMetrics handle game and field scheduling?**
It has scheduling tools, including field plans and a shared calendar of games, practices, and events. Whether they fit a shared-field, interleague, volunteer-umpire baseball league is something to test in a demo with your own season, not assume.

**What should we do first?**
Export your data from Sports Connect, ask your District Administrator when your league moves to PlayMetrics, and put "scheduling plan for 2027" on your next board agenda as its own line item, separate from the registration migration.
