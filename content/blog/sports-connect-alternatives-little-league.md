# Sports Connect Alternatives in 2026: What Little League Admins Actually Need to Know

*Last updated: July 2026*

If you run a Little League and you've heard that Sports Connect is going away, you've probably started searching for alternatives. Here's the thing most "Sports Connect alternatives" articles get wrong: **if you're a chartered Little League, the biggest part of that decision has already been made for you.**

This guide explains what's actually happening, what the transition does and doesn't solve, and what your real options are — written for the volunteer board member who has a league to run and no time for vendor marketing.

## What actually happened

Three facts drive everything else:

1. **In June 2025, Stack Sports (Sports Connect's parent company) merged with PlayMetrics**, a youth sports operations platform that built its reputation running competitive soccer clubs. Private equity firm Genstar Capital backed the merger and is the majority owner of the combined company, with PlayMetrics' CEO leading it. ([Announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250611023323/en/PlayMetrics-and-Stack-Sports-Combine-to-Create-Leader-in-Sports-Software))

2. **The combined company announced that Sports Connect will be sunset in 2027.** The platform thousands of leagues have used for registration, websites, and league management is being retired.

3. **Little League International evaluated both platforms and chose PlayMetrics.** Central Registration will run through PlayMetrics beginning with the 2027 season, with leagues managing player registration, league operations, and volunteer management there. This was significant enough to get its own education session at the 2026 Little League International Congress. ([Little League Congress details](https://www.littleleague.org/news/looking-back-at-day-two-of-the-2026-little-league-international-congress/), [Sports Connect's Little League page](https://sportsconnect.com/little-league/))

AYSO is making the same move on the soccer side — its regions are also being transitioned to PlayMetrics. ([AYSO announcement](https://ayso.org/ayso-registration-and-region-management-with-playmetrics/))

## What this means if you're a chartered Little League

Because registration for Little League flows through Central Registration, and Central Registration is moving to PlayMetrics, **your registration path for 2027 is essentially decided.** Unlike an independent rec league that can shop the whole market, a chartered Little League isn't really choosing between six registration platforms. Confirm specifics with your District Administrator, but the practical picture is:

- **Registration, volunteer management, and background-check workflows** move to PlayMetrics for the 2027 season.
- **Your league website**, if it lives on Sports Connect today, needs a plan — either PlayMetrics' equivalent or a standalone site.
- **Your historical data** (past rosters, registrations) needs to be exported before the lights go out. Do this early, during an off-season, not in a panic during signups.

So the honest question for a Little League admin isn't *"what replaces Sports Connect?"* It's narrower and more useful: **"the registration decision is made — what about everything else?"**

## The part the transition doesn't automatically solve: your season

Registration is two months of pain a year. The other pain runs all season long, and it's the part Sports Connect was never great at anyway:

- **Field conflicts.** Four divisions, two shared parks, one city permit schedule. One double-booked field ruins a Saturday for eighty families.
- **Interleague play.** Coordinating schedules with neighboring leagues, where every change has to be re-agreed by two boards.
- **Umpire assignments.** Who's behind the plate for the 12s on Sunday, and did anyone tell them the game moved?
- **Rainouts.** One storm and you're rebuilding three weeks of schedule by hand.
- **Snack shack duty.** The rotation nobody wants to manage and everybody complains about.
- **Playoff brackets.** Built in a spreadsheet at 11pm, usually.

PlayMetrics is a serious platform — its own materials describe strength in club operations, and it has been expanding into leagues and governing bodies. But its roots are in competitive soccer club management, which is a different shape of problem from a volunteer-run baseball league juggling shared fields, umpires, and interleague agreements. Maybe it covers your scheduling needs well; maybe it doesn't. **The point is to evaluate that question separately instead of assuming the registration migration solves it.** Ask for a demo of the scheduling workflow specifically, using your real scenario: your fields, your divisions, your interleague partners.

## Your three realistic paths

### Path 1: Move to PlayMetrics and use it for everything

The default path, and the least work. If PlayMetrics' scheduling handles your league's reality — shared fields across divisions, interleague, officials, rainout rescheduling — this is the simplest answer. Pressure-test it in a demo before your board assumes it. Costs and packaging for Little League–sized organizations are worth asking about directly, since published pricing has historically been geared to clubs.

### Path 2: All-in-one alternatives (mostly for non-chartered organizations)

If you're an independent league — not bound to Little League Central Registration — the full-replacement market is real and competitive. The commonly compared options are **TeamSnap, SportsEngine, LeagueApps, Jersey Watch, TeamLinkt, and TeamSideline**, which each combine registration, payments, websites, scheduling, and communication with different strengths and price points. ([One roundup of these options](https://www.jerseywatch.com/blog/sportsconnect-alternatives))

Rough honest sorting, worth verifying against current pricing:

- **TeamSnap / SportsEngine** — biggest brands, strong parent-facing apps, per-participant or subscription costs that add up for large leagues.
- **LeagueApps** — powerful and configurable, aimed at larger multi-program organizations; likely more platform than a single-town league needs.
- **Jersey Watch / TeamLinkt / TeamSideline** — leaner, volunteer-friendly, budget-conscious options; TeamLinkt in particular markets a free tier.

For a chartered Little League, these can still make sense for the *website and communication* pieces — but they can't replace Central Registration, so evaluate them for what they'd actually do for you, not the whole bundle.

### Path 3: Keep registration on the mandated path, add a dedicated scheduling layer

The third option treats the 2027 transition as a chance to unbundle: let PlayMetrics do what Little League requires (registration, volunteers, compliance), and run scheduling — the all-season pain — in a tool built specifically for it.

## A sane timeline for the transition

- **Now – fall 2026:** Export your historical data from Sports Connect. Ask your DA what the PlayMetrics migration includes and when your league gets access. Demo the scheduling workflow with your real fields and divisions.
- **Fall 2026 (Fall Ball, if you play it):** Lowest-stakes window to trial any new scheduling tool — smaller rosters, fewer teams, real games.
- **Winter 2026–27:** Decide your website plan and your scheduling plan *before* spring registration opens. Train the two or three volunteers who'll actually run these tools.
- **Spring 2027:** First season fully off Sports Connect. Everything should already be boring by then.

## FAQ

**Is Sports Connect really shutting down?**
Yes. Following the 2025 Stack Sports–PlayMetrics merger, the company announced Sports Connect and the Association Platform will be sunset in 2027.

**Do Little Leagues get to choose their registration platform?**
Effectively no — Little League Central Registration moves to PlayMetrics beginning with the 2027 season. Confirm details with your District Administrator.

**Does the PlayMetrics transition handle my game and field scheduling?**
That's the question to ask in a demo. Registration and volunteer management are the announced scope; whether its scheduling fits a shared-field, interleague, volunteer-umpire baseball league is something to verify against your own season, not assume.

**What should we do first?**
Export your data from Sports Connect, and put "scheduling plan for 2027" on your next board agenda as its own line item — separate from the registration migration.
