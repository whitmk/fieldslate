Public league schedule — mockup

One link per league that families open and a league can embed on its own website. Games are colored by where they're played: Home = at one of the league's own parks, Away = anywhere else. Every game also carries a Home/Away text tag, and the two differ in shape (solid vs. outlined), so color is never the only signal.

Made-up league, teams and parks. Page address in this mockup: thefieldslate.com/s/<token>.

1 · Desktop — list view (the default)

Shown: upcoming games, every locked division. The rained-out game stays in place, struck through. An interleague away game shows the other league's field as they typed it. A reschedule-pending game stays at its current time with a "Time may change" tag.
