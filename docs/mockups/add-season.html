MOCKUP — Add season, every state, desktop and 375px. Static, no data. Prices and the “what you lose” line are filled in from one config block at the bottom, never typed into a screen.

Add season

What each league sees when it starts a new season and can’t create one free. The server decides which screen applies (begin_season_purchase); the page only renders it. The example league is on Elite with “Fall 2026” active, adding “Spring 27” (Apr 3 – May 22, 2027).

Screen 1 · paid league, a season active

Add season

Every new season is paid for, at the league’s current plan. No plan choice here and no “upgrade” wording. The season is created only after payment clears.

Screen 2 · paid league, nothing active

Tier choice, with the “what you lose” line

With no active season the league picks the plan for this one — up or down. Shown with Pro picked on an Elite league, so the line appears; with Elite picked it isn’t shown. The line lists only what the code actually turns off, built from the plan limits and the Elite-only pages.

Screen 3 · Free league, its season active

Upgrade this season

Free gets one active season at a time. To get another, the league first upgrades the one it has, in place (nothing is copied or lost). After that, new seasons are bought at the new plan. “Upgrade” is the right word here — it is the only screen that uses it. No season form: nothing new is created.

Screen 4 · comped league

Billing managed by FieldSlate

A comped league never reaches checkout. One plain sentence, the contact link, and a way out. No price, no plan names.

Screen 5 · after Stripe

Success page — waiting

Stripe sends the buyer back before our webhook has necessarily run. The page reads the purchase and waits for it; it never assumes the season exists yet.

Success page — ready

The purchase is completed and the season exists.

Success page — flagged

The payment arrived but didn’t match what was expected (a second tab, a plan that changed mid-checkout, an old checkout). The money is never lost: it becomes a season credit (or, for the $100 upgrade, is held for a refund), FieldSlate is emailed at once, and the page says so without blaming anyone. No season is created automatically.

Success page — taking longer than usual

After about 30 seconds with no answer. Honest, no promise we can’t keep, no retry button that could look like “pay again”.

Where the numbers come from. In the app these come from one shared config: prices from SEASON_PRICE_USD (src/lib/stripe.ts, checked against the live Stripe prices), limits from PLAN_LIMITS, the Elite-only pages from one feature list beside it. This mockup copies them into the block below and fills every screen from it.
