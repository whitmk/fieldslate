Playoff series

Each round of a single-elimination bracket — including Stage 2 of a two-stage setup — can be 1 game, best of 3, best of 5 or best of 7. A team moves on when it wins the series. Games past the minimum are marked if needed; when a series is decided they are cancelled automatically, and clearing a result that un-decides it brings them back. The higher seed (from the seeding list) is home in games 1, 3, 5 and 7. Each screen is shown at desktop width (left) and on a 375px phone (right). Narrow your window below 1000px to see the phone frames only.

Example used throughout: Majors, 6 teams — 1 Expos, 2 Bears, 3 Lions, 4 Mets, 5 Pirates, 6 Royals. Round 1 is single games (seeds 1 and 2 have byes). The Semifinals and the Final are best of 3. Majors games are 120 minutes; 1 game per team per day; playing days Sat and Sun.

Results go in order

Game 3 can't take a result until Game 2 has one

On the card, Result is offered only on the next game of an undecided series. A stale tab that opens a later game anyway gets the refusal in the modal (the database refuses too). A rained-out game is moved with Move — it stays the next game until it has a result.

Public league schedule (/s/…) and its calendar feed

"Game 2 of 3", "If needed" — and no not-needed games at all

Same rows as today. Series games read "Semifinal 1 · Game 2 of 3"; an undecided series' extra games carry an If needed tag. Not-needed games are left out of the page and the all-games calendar feed (Semifinal 2's Game 3 doesn't appear). No scores, as today. The home team is listed first. A series whose teams aren't both known yet names no home team: its games read "Bears and Winner of Semifinal 1" (no team listed first as home), here, in the feed and in the CSV. The Home/Away tag under the field is the park, as today, and shows on every row. Home for every game of a series is decided once both teams are set (by seeding or by a result) — the higher seed in games 1, 3, 5 and 7.

Before the build

Decided 2026-10-20, and the questions this mockup raises

Decided: series length per round (1, 3, 5, 7); strict alternation, higher true seed home in odd games, decided for the whole series once both teams are set (until then no home team is shown on the public page, feed or CSV; the park Home/Away tag always shows); if-needed games dated at generation; results in order (rainouts are moved); one card per series in the tree and print; "Winner of Semifinal 1" only when the feeding round is a series; a new not-needed status; not-needed games hidden from the public page and feeds; the feed cut-off and auto-archive ignore them; no series in double elimination; built in steps with holds, one go-live.

  1. Game numbers for series games. The mockup gives series games no "Game N" of their own — they read "Semifinal 1 · Game 2 of 3" — and "Game N" counts single games only, so single-game rounds keep 1, 2, 3… with no jumps (here Round 1 is Games 1–2).
    Alternative: number every game in one sequence (the Semifinals would be Games 3–8) — longer labels, and a single-game Final after series would read "Game 9". Recommend single games only.
  2. Names for series in early rounds. Final, Semifinal 1–2, Quarterfinal 1–4 are shown. Rounds before the quarterfinals would read "Round 1 · Series 3".
    Recommend as stated.
  3. Series score on the public page. Today the public page shows no scores, so the mockup doesn't show "Expos lead 1–0" there.
    Recommend leaving scores admin-only for now.
  4. If-needed games hold the field, the teams and the snack shack. Until a series is decided, its if-needed games count as booked: the field is busy for other bookings, the teams count as playing that day, and the snack shack treats them like any playoff game. Once not needed, all three are released.
    Recommend as stated.
  5. The championship banner. For a series Final: "Expos win the Final 2–1".
    Recommend.
  6. Changing series lengths later. Edit setup can change a round's length only while the bracket has no results (today's rebuild rule).
    Recommend as stated.
