Mockup — APPROVED 2026-10-20 — public bracket page per division. Made-up league, teams and parks.

Public bracket page

One read-only page per division's playoffs, at thefieldslate.com/s/<token>/bracket/<division> — the league's public schedule link plus the division. The public schedule's on/off switch and Reset govern it. Desktop opens on the bracket tree, a phone on the list. It refreshes itself every 60 seconds while the tab is visible.

Screen 1 · Playoffs page (admin)

A "Public page" strip on every bracket card

The card's header is unchanged (View bracket · Export · Edit setup · Delete). Below it, one strip: Copy public link, Open ↗, and the Show scores publicly switch (default on). The switch saves at once — it is NOT in Edit setup, because Edit setup is refused once results exist and scores can be turned off at any time. When the bracket can't be shared, the strip says why in one line, with a link to fix it, and the copy button is disabled. A two-stage pair is one card, one strip, one switch for both stages.

Screen 5 · Public page, round robin then bracket

Stage 1 games with results; Stage 2 with seed placeholders. No records table.

One page for the pair. Stage 1 lists its games by round with scores (or winners only) and who sits out. Stage 2 is the bracket tree / list, reading "Seed 1"–"Seed 4" until the league seeds it, then team names with their seed. There is no records table and no standings on this page, in either stage — that box stays admin-only. The one line under Stage 1 says how many move on, not who.
