Two-stage playoffs

Stage 1 is one round robin. Stage 2 is a single-elimination bracket the admin seeds by hand once Stage 1 is done. FieldSlate shows each team's round-robin record as reference and never ranks them. Each screen is shown at desktop width (left) and on a 375px phone (right); the two are the same screen. Narrow your window below 1000px to see the phone frames only.

Example used throughout: Majors has 8 teams. The Cubs opted out, so 7 play the round robin (21 games). The top 6 advance to an 8-slot bracket, so seeds 1 and 2 get byes. Majors games are 120 minutes.

Stage 1 results and the seeds are locked

Once any bracket game has a result, the database refuses to edit or clear a round-robin result, and refuses to re-seed. The way back is clearing the bracket's results (latest round first, as today).

Before the build

Decisions — approved 2026-10-19 as recommended (D3 changed to "Seed anyway")

Approved by Whit on 2026-10-19 with one change (D3).

  1. Game numbers across the two stages. Bracket games are numbered by bracket position starting at 1 (the advancement rule and its SQL copy depend on it), and round-robin games also start at 1. The mockup shows round-robin games as "RR Game 13" so "Game 2" and "Winner of Game 2" always mean the bracket.
    Alternative: renumber the bracket after the round robin (Games 22–28) — touches the advancement rule in TypeScript and SQL. Recommend "RR Game N".
  2. Bye games leave gaps. With byes, the bracket has no Game 1 or Game 3 (today's single-elimination numbering). Families see Games 2, 4, 5, 6, 7.
    Recommend leaving it — same as every bracket today.
  3. A rained-out round-robin game that's never made up. DECIDED 2026-10-19: "Seed anyway" (screens 6b, 8b). Offered once every game without a result is rained out or dated before today; the confirm names them, records leave them out, and it is logged.
  4. Deleting. The two stages are one setup: Delete on the card removes both, and the confirm counts both stages' games. Neither stage can be deleted alone.
    Recommend both-or-nothing.
  5. Edit setup after games exist. Rebuild is refused today once any game has a result. With two stages: changing Stage 2's dates or fields stays allowed until Stage 2 has a result; changing the team list or the advancing count is refused once Stage 1 has a result.
    Recommend as stated.
  6. The advancing count after seeding. Fixed at setup; Change seeds can reorder but not change how many advance (the bracket's shape and dates depend on it).
    Recommend fixed; changing it means Edit setup, which rebuilds Stage 2 while it has no results.
  7. Who seeded, and the "Seeds are still right" click. Both go in the activity log with the seed order.
    Recommend logging both.
